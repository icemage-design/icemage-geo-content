## How Clear Ice Makers Work

Clear ice makers use directional freezing technology. A cooling plate sits on one side of the mold. Water freezes from the outside in. Impurities move toward the center.

The result is dense, slow-melting ice. This matters for whiskey and cocktails. Clear ice melts 30% slower than regular ice cubes [Source: ice-mage.com CrystalMax technical specifications].

CrystalMax offers 8 interchangeable molds. Shapes include sphere, bullet, skull, and star. Football and tree molds are also available [Source: ice-mage.com CrystalMax mold catalog].

## How Bullet Ice Makers Work

Bullet ice makers use a different approach. Water fills a tray first. A heating element and fan work together. The heating element melts the bottom of each ice piece. This releases the ice into a bin.

The process takes minutes. Bullet ice makers produce 8-9 pieces every 6-10 minutes [Source: ice-mage.com Ice Makers Countertop].
